# Forderbar

**Inkasso für Verbraucher** · Working domain: `forderbar.de`

AI-assisted consumer-side recovery of clearly documented monetary claims against companies.

## Product principles

- Free customer account and document upload.
- Full pre-screening **before any outgoing demand**: legal plausibility, evidence, debtor identification, solvency signals, collectability and unit economics.
- Decline unsuitable claims with understandable reasons; route uncertain cases to qualified human review.
- Consumer accepts a legally reviewed collection mandate when opening an accepted case.
- Initial plain-language payment demand in consumer's name, issued only after their approval.
- Delivery via verified email, fax or postal channel; retain delivery records and monitor permitted communications and payment confirmations.
- Professional collection only when current claim, maturity, default, licensing and fee requirements are satisfied.
- Payment is made by the debtor to an account designated by Forderbar; Forderbar forwards the recovered principal minus its success fee to the consumer, with a statement (see AGB §5). Handling and segregation of third-party funds is subject to legal review. Direct payment to the consumer is the exception and must be reported.
- Success target: >=80% full recovery among accepted, matured cases, **not a proven performance claim**.
- Consumer pricing proposal: 0% commission for claims up to €100; 10% of the whole recovered principal for claims above €100 (from €100.01) on success, subject to fee and consumer-law review. Statutory collection costs paid by the debtor are kept by Forderbar in addition to the commission and are not charged to the consumer (AGB §5(4)); lawyer review pending.

## Initial claim categories

Online retail refunds, subscriptions, telecom credits, energy refunds; selected travel reimbursements and straightforward automotive purchase refunds. Exclude technical workmanship and disputed car-repair quality claims.

## Release restrictions

The project must not send real collection demands, accept binding Inkasso mandates, or claim to be a registered debt collection provider until appropriate legal review, RDG registration/qualified supervision, professional insurance, compliant contracts and data protections are verified. Domain/brand clearance for **Forderbar** also remains open. The expression *Inkasso für Verbraucher* describes the service and is not a verified trademark clearance.

## Development

A first preview implementation is planned. Production and real-money flows remain disabled until authorization and verification.
